Compound Notes is written by one person, not a media company — someone who spent their early twenties assuming investing was for people with more money, more time, or more financial background than they had. It turned out none of those things were actually required to start. That gap between "I should probably learn this" and "I actually did something about it" is what this site is for.

**Who this is for:** people in their late teens through thirties who want a plain-language answer to "okay, but what do I actually do" — college students wondering if it's worth investing $20 a month, people who finally have a little disposable income and don't know where it should go, anyone who's tired of finance content that's either oversimplified motivational posters or dense enough to need a glossary.

**What this isn't:** financial advice, a signal service, or a place that tells you which stock to buy. Nobody writing here is a licensed financial advisor. Treat everything here as a starting point for your own research, not a instruction manual.

**How the site works:** new pieces get added regularly, organized as straightforward articles rather than a feed of hot takes. Some articles mention specific brokerages or tools by name, including ones with referral programs — when that happens, it's disclosed plainly, both on the [disclosure page]({{ '/disclosure/' | relative_url }}) and right next to the link itself, not buried somewhere you'd have to go looking for it.

If something here is wrong, unclear, or you think it's missing an important caveat, that's useful to know — this site would rather be corrected than keep an error up for pageviews.
