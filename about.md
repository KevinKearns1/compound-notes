I'm not a media company, just one person — I spent my early twenties assuming investing was for people with more money, more time, or more financial background than I had. It turned out none of those things were actually required to start. That gap between "I should probably learn this" and "I actually did something about it" is what this site is for.

**Who this is for:** people in their late teens through thirties who want a plain-language answer to "okay, but what do I actually do" — college students wondering if it's worth investing $20 a month, people who finally have a little disposable income and don't know where it should go, anyone who's tired of finance content that's either oversimplified motivational posters or dense enough to need a glossary.

**What this isn't:** financial advice, a signal service, or a place that tells you which stock to buy. I'm not a licensed financial advisor. Treat everything here as a starting point for your own research, not an instruction manual.

**How this works:** I add new pieces regularly, written as straightforward articles rather than a feed of hot takes. Some mention specific brokerages or tools by name, including ones with referral programs — when that happens, I disclose it plainly, both on the [disclosure page]({{ '/disclosure/' | relative_url }}) and right next to the link itself, not buried somewhere you'd have to go looking for it.

If something here is wrong, unclear, or missing an important caveat, tell me — I'd rather be corrected than keep an error up for pageviews.
